Direct Point-to-Point Architecture

QRFlux uses zero cloud servers. Transfers stream directly through your local Wi-Fi router or phone hotspot at native device hardware speeds.

1
Stage Your Files
Drop documents, 4K videos, zip archives, or photos into the Web Sender. File checksums are calculated instantly.
2
Scan QR on Android
Open the QRFlux Android app and tap Scan QR Code. The app reads the ephemeral session key and local IP in 0.2s.
3
Stream at Full Speed
The transfer streams over raw high-performance TCP sockets (20-50 MB/s). Saved straight into your Android Downloads folder!
